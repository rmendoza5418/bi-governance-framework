# Onboarding & Developer Tracks

A CoE that only publishes standards but doesn't invest in training will watch adoption stall at 40%. The onboarding and champion programs are what turn written standards into lived practice.

---

## New User Onboarding (Consumer Track)

For business users who will view dashboards but not build them.

**Week 1 — Orientation (1 hour)**
- Where to find certified content (`[Certified]` project)
- How to tell certified vs. uncertified content apart
- How to subscribe to a dashboard for email delivery
- How to report an accuracy issue (contact the data steward, not IT)

**Week 2 — Self-Service Basics (2 hours, optional)**
- How to use filters and parameters
- How to download data from a view
- How to create a simple custom view without publishing

**Completion:** User receives "BI Platform Consumer" badge in HRIS

---

## Developer Onboarding (Creator Track)

For business analysts and BI developers who will build and publish content.

**Phase 1 — Standards (Self-paced, ~3 hours)**
- [ ] Read naming conventions and complete 10-question quiz
- [ ] Review project structure and permission model
- [ ] Review performance guidelines
- [ ] Complete Tableau performance training (internal or Tableau eLearning)

**Phase 2 — Sandbox Publish (Week 1–2)**
- [ ] Publish first workbook to `[Sandbox]`
- [ ] Shadow a BI Champion through a dashboard review
- [ ] Complete a peer review of another new developer's sandbox workbook

**Phase 3 — First Certification (Week 3–4)**
- [ ] Submit first workbook for Silver certification
- [ ] Address reviewer feedback and resubmit if needed
- [ ] Attend first CoE monthly meeting

**Completion:** User promoted to "BI Developer" role in Tableau Server; can publish to `[Reviewed]` after Champion approval

## BI Champion Program

Champions are the CoE's force multipliers — domain-embedded BI experts who bridge the gap between the central CoE and business units.

**Selection criteria:**
- Active Tableau Creator with at least 1 Silver-certified workbook
- Respected in their business unit (peers seek them out for BI help)
- Committed to 3–5 hours/month for CoE activities

**Champion responsibilities:**
- First-line Silver certification reviews for their domain
- Monthly check-in with their domain's active developers
- Attend monthly CoE champion sync
- Surface new data needs and governance gaps to the CoE Lead
- Run quarterly "BI office hours" for their team

**Champion support from CoE:**
- Priority access to CoE Lead for consultation
- First access to new platform features and beta tools
- Recognition in leadership reporting
- Budget for Tableau training and certification (Tableau Desktop Specialist, etc.)

**Champion-to-developer ratio target:** 1 champion per 15–20 active developers

## CoE Monthly Agenda Template

```
BI CoE Monthly Meeting — [Month Year]
Duration: 60 minutes

1. Metrics review (10 min)
   - Active users (MTD vs. prior month)
   - Certifications completed
   - Stale content removed
   - Extract refresh SLA compliance

2. New certifications (10 min)
   - Workbooks promoted to Gold since last meeting
   - Workbooks decertified / archived

3. Platform updates (10 min)
   - New features or version upgrades
   - Data source library additions

4. Champion updates (15 min)
   - Round-robin: what's your domain building?
   - Escalations or blockers

5. Standards evolution (10 min)
   - Any standards that aren't working in practice?
   - Proposals for updates

6. Open Q&A (5 min)

Action items: [owner] [due date]
```
